==History of North Africa (3500 B.C. - 1500 A.D.)==
=== Ancient Egypt ===
''Main articles: [[History of Ancient Egypt]], [[Kush]]''

Africa's earliest evidence of written history was in [[Ancient Egypt]], and the [[Egyptian calendar]] is still used as the standard for dating [[bronze age]] and [[iron age]] cultures throughout the region.

In about 3100 B.C., [[Egypt]] was united under a ruler known as Mena, or [[Menes]], who inaugurated the first of the 30 dynasties into which Egypt's ancient history is divided: the [[Old Kingdom|Old]], [[Middle Kingdom of Egypt|Middle Kingdom]]s and the [[New Kingdom of Egypt|New Kingdom]].
